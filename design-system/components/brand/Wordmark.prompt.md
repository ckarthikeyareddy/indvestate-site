The INDVESTATE wordmark and its three lockups — use wherever the brand signs a surface (nav, end-cards, posts, cover).

```jsx
<Wordmark size={40} />
<Wordmark lockup="tagline" size={56} />
<Wordmark lockup="city" city="HYDERABAD" size={28} />
<Wordmark lockup="status" status={<StatusPill kind="live-drop" />} size={24} />
<Wordmark plate size={32} /> {/* over footage */}
```

- Never outline, glow, stretch, recolour to saffron or set lowercase.
- Clear space = height of the "I" on all sides. Minimum 24px cap height (size ≈ 34) when it stands alone; nav may go to 20.
