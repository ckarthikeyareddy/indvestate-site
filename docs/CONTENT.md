B01 · BOOKING MONEY · "Paid in full. No possession."
TG RERA imposed a ₹ 32.17 lakh penalty on Janapriya Projects for collecting booking money
before registering the project (Sitara at Janapriya Lake Front), and ordered 10.70%
interest to the buyers for the delay. Order dated 18 Sept 2026.
Check we run: the project's RERA number on the portal before any money moves.
Source: NewsMeter, 20 Sept 2026, https://newsmeter.in/hyderabad/tg-rera-imposes-rs-3217-lakh-penalty-on-janapriya-projects-orders-1070-interest-to-home-buyers-775803

B02 · THE 10 PER CENT LINE · "Twenty-five per cent, no agreement."
In Aug 2026 TG-RERA penalised four projects: AV Infracon for sales without registration
(two projects), Adhuri Infra ₹ 6.78 lakh for collecting 25% of the price without a
registered Agreement for Sale, and SVM Aditya Homes ₹ 43.16 lakh (Tech Homes) for sales
without registration and without HMDA approvals. The rule: no more than 10% before a
registered agreement.
Check we run: the agreement for sale is registered before the 10% line.
Source: The Hans India, 13 Aug 2026, https://www.thehansindia.com/news/cities/hyderabad/tg-rera-acts-tough-slaps-heavy-penalty-on-rogue-developers-in-city-1108837

B03 · PRE-LAUNCH · "Kompally, ₹ 4.74 crore."
TG RERA fined Bharathi Builders ₹ 4,74,17,729 for collecting money through pre-launch
offers on Bharathi Lake View Apartments, Kompally, without registration; the project never
started; the company was declared a defaulter promoter and ordered to refund within 60
days. (Relevant because we list in Kompally.)
Check we run: we do not take or pass on any pre-launch money, ever.
Source: Siasat, Mar 2026, https://www.siasat.com/tg-rera-fines-bharathi-builders-rs-4-74-crore-for-illegal-pre-launch-offer-3434951/

B04 · PLOTS · "HMDA approval is not RERA registration."
TG RERA ordered penalty proceedings against Vajra Prekon Estates for marketing and selling
plots in a project that had HMDA approval but no RERA registration; holding development
rights made it the promoter under the Act.
Check we run: layout approval and RERA registration are two different documents; we need
both on file.
Source: NewsMeter, 13 July 2026, https://newsmeter.in/hyderabad/tgrera-orders-penalty-proceedings-against-vajra-prekon-for-selling-plots-in-unregistered-project-771575

B05 · BUFFER ZONE · "Inside the lake's line."
Since HYDRAA was formed in July 2024, structures inside Full Tank Level and buffer zones
have been demolished regardless of approvals shown by the seller; the N-Convention case
(Aug 2024) involved 1 acre 12 guntas in FTL and 2 acres 18 guntas in buffer zone with no
building permission.
Check we run: an FTL/buffer overlay on every parcel before we walk it.
Sources: ETV Bharat, 24 Aug 2024, https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/hyderabad-commissioner-on-demolition-of-structures-of-n-convention-enn24082404483 · Probity guide, 3 May 2026, https://www.probitypm.in/blog-hydraa-ftl-map-verification-hyderabad-2026.html
