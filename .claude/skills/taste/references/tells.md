# AI tells - the full sweep list

Patterns the model reaches for when it tries to "look designed". Hard bans unless the brief explicitly asks. Use for the final sweep on any shipped page, image set, or board. SKILL.md 2.9 is the short form; this is the long form.

---

## Hero and top of page
- Version labels as eyebrows: `V0.6`, `BETA`, `INVITE-ONLY PREVIEW`, `EARLY ACCESS`, `ALPHA` (only when the brief is literally a launch).
- "Brand · No. 01 · The 6-quart" micro-meta sub-eyebrows.
- Tiny tagline below the CTAs ("Works with GitHub, GitLab, and self-hosted"); trust micro-strip ("Used by teams at..."); pricing teaser; feature bullets; social-proof avatar row inside the hero. All move below the hero.
- Logo wall inside the hero. It goes directly under.
- Hero top padding beyond `pt-24`; hero content floating halfway down the viewport.
- Text + gradient blob with no real visual.
- Reflexive left-text / right-image; reflexive centered-text-over-dark-mesh.
- 4+ line headlines; 6-line narrow-container headlines.

## Section numbering and micro-labels
- `00 / INDEX`, `001 · Capabilities`, `06 · how it works`, `SECTION 04`, `QUESTION 05`, `ABOUT US` as labels.
- `01 / 4` pagination on images or bento tiles.
- `Scroll · 001 Capabilities` scroll cues.
- "Index of Work, 2018 - 2026" range eyebrows.
- An eyebrow above every section header (cap: 1 per 3 sections).
- "Stage 1 / Step 2 / Phase 03 / Pass One" step labels.
- Micro-meta sentences under eyebrows ("Each of these is a feature we ship today, not a roadmap promise.").

## Separators, dots, dashes
- Em-dash `—` anywhere visible; en-dash `–` as a separator. Only `-` and the math minus.
- Middle dot `·` more than once per line; as the universal separator.
- Colored status dots before nav items, list rows, badges without real semantic state (max one per section when real).
- `<br>`-broken italicized headlines as a default move ("for thirty<br>*years.*").
- One random serif word in a sans headline; one-word accent color in a headline.
- Vertical rotated text.
- Crosshair / hairline grid lines as decoration (allowed only inside the brutalist preset where they are structural).
- Giant outline numerals as decoration.

## Fake product previews
- Div-built fake task lists, terminals, dashboards, chat windows in the hero or anywhere.
- Fake version footers inside fake screenshots (`v0.6.2-rc.1`, `last sync 4s ago · main`).
- Fake KPI strips (99% satisfaction, $10 saved, infinity scale) unless real KPIs were supplied.
- Charts and sparklines on non-analytics sites.
- Progress bars with filled background tracks as comparison visuals.

## Copy tells
- "Quietly in use at" / "Quietly trusted by".
- "From the field", "Field notes", "Currently on the bench", "On our desks", "Loose plates" style poetic section labels.
- "We respect the French ones" mock-humble asides.
- Weather / locale / time strips ("LIS 14:23 · 18°C", "Lisbon, working with founders") unless the brand is genuinely place- or timezone-based.
- "Reservation 412 of 800" live counters as decoration.
- Elevate, seamless, unleash, next-gen, game-changer, delve, revolutionize, transformative, tapestry, "in the world of", "unlock your potential", "smarter than ever", "transform your day".
- Acme, Nexus, SmartFlow, NovaCore, Flowbit, Quantumly, Quantix, VeloPay, Cloudly; John Doe, Jane Smith, Sarah Chan, Jack Su.
- `99.99%`, `50%`, `$100.00`, `1234567`; fake-precise engineering specs the brand never claimed.
- Lorem ipsum; identical blog dates; one avatar reused; Title Case Everything; "Oops!"; exclamation marks in success states; passive voice.
- Cute-but-wrong wordplay, forced metaphors, performatively thoughtful micro-copy ("free on its past", "elegant nothing").
- Duplicate CTA intents ("Get in touch" + "Let's talk" + "Start a project").
- CTA labels that wrap to two lines at desktop.

## Pills, labels, captions, stamps
- Pills / tags overlaid on photos (`Brand · 02`, `PLATE · BRAND`).
- Photo-credit captions as decoration (`Field study no. 12 · Ines Caetano`, `Frame XII · 35mm`); real credits for real photographers only.
- Version footers on marketing pages (`v1.4.2`, `Build 0048`).
- Decoration text strips at hero bottom (`BRAND. MOTION. SPATIAL.`, `TYPE / FORM / MOTION`, `ESTD. 2018 · LISBON`).
- Floating top-right explainer paragraph in a section header.
- Pseudo-system labels ("00 orchestration layer", runtime markers, operator / control-room jargon) outside the brutalist preset.
- Pill-shaped "New" / "Beta" badges by default.

## Layout tells
- Three identical feature cards in a row.
- Zigzag image / text splits three or more in a row.
- The same layout family twice on one page.
- Split-header: big headline left, small paragraph floating right.
- Bento with empty cells, or all white-on-white text cells.
- Cards inside cards inside cards; a giant rounded wrapper around a whole section.
- Border + shadow + white card for everything.
- `border-t` + `border-b` on every row of a long list; 10-row spec tables with hairlines.
- 20-row data tables on a marketing page.
- Everything centered and symmetrical; symmetrical vertical padding by default.
- `height: 100vh` full-height sections.
- Dashboard always with a left sidebar.
- Accordion FAQ by default; 3-card testimonial carousel with dots; 3-tower pricing with the middle one taller; modals for every action; circle avatars only; sun / moon toggle; 4-column footer farm.
- A dark section sandwiched in a light page (or vice versa).
- Horizontal overflow on mobile.

## Visual and color tells
- AI purple / violet / lila glow; purple-to-blue and pink-to-orange gradients; mesh-blob backgrounds; neon outer glows; gradient text as "premium".
- Cream `#F4F1EA`-family + high-contrast serif + terracotta / clay / brass / oxblood + espresso text for any premium-consumer brief (rotate palettes; only on explicit brand ask).
- Near-black + one acid green or vermilion accent as the reflexive "dark premium" default.
- Pure `#000000` / `#ffffff` surfaces; mixed warm and cool grays; more than one accent; oversaturated accents; generic black drop shadows; inconsistent light direction; textureless flat vectors; even 45-degree linear gradients.
- Glassmorphism stacked without reason; floating spheres / blobs / orbs; over-rendered noise hiding the layout; random futuristic details.
- Inter / Roboto / Arial / Open Sans / Helvetica as the default; Fraunces / Instrument Serif as the reflexive "editorial" serif; the same serif two projects in a row.
- Lucide / Feather / FontAwesome / Material icons as the default; mixed stroke weights; hand-rolled icon paths; hand-rolled decorative SVG illustrations.
- Rocket for "launch", shield for "security", generic "diverse team" stock photos, Unsplash hotlinks, same stock silhouette repeated.
- Custom cursors. Emojis in UI, code, alt text. Oversized radii on everything; mixed radius systems.
- Default shadcn/ui state; default Tailwind shadows (`shadow-md`, `shadow-lg`); arbitrary `z-50` / `z-[9999]`.

## Motion tells
- Fade-and-slide-up on every section; hover scale on every card with no hierarchy reason.
- GSAP everywhere because GSAP is available; more than one marquee per page.
- Infinite micro-loops on informational cards.
- `linear` / `ease-in-out`; instant state changes; animating `top` / `left` / `width` / `height`.
- `window.addEventListener('scroll')`; `window.scrollY` in React state; rAF loops touching state.
- Sticky-stack triggers at `top center` / `top 80%` (half-scroll bug); horizontal pan starting before pin; missing `useEffect` cleanups.
- Claimed motion that never renders; motion that ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Scroll cues: "Scroll", "↓ scroll", "Scroll to explore", animated mouse-wheel icons, bouncing chevrons.

## Mobile-specific tells
- Phone-shaped website; website hero inside a phone frame.
- Fake fintech chart dashboards; 12 widgets fighting on a home screen; repeated stat cards.
- Cloned onboarding slides with a swapped icon; motivational filler; early rating prompts.
- Content in unsafe areas; overloaded bottom nav; every action equal weight.
- Tiny text; too much above the fold; floating surfaces everywhere; 5 levels of framing.
- Sterile flat backgrounds with no atmosphere; generic palettes; Lucide-like icon defaults.
- Inconsistent device mockups: mixed bezels, uneven margins, phone touching the edge, frame dominating the content.
- Screen sets drifting into different design systems.

## Image / board tells
- One tall compressed image for a whole page; a collage instead of one image per section; "one best image" and skipping the rest.
- Cropping a previous render for a detail view instead of regenerating.
- Tiny unreadable text in comps; tiny thumbnails; one hero image then text forever.
- Random floating icons on brand boards; fake tiny UI; inconsistent logo variants; too many colors; cheap neon; PowerPoint-looking panels; soulless dashboards as "applications".
- Stock people, office photos, robot imagery, unrelated scenery.
- Real-brand logos copied into an invented brand's board.
